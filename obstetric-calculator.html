Introduction

This Privacy Policy describing how the “Obstetric Calculator” (Kalkulator Obstetri) application handles data. Our application is a professional clinical tool designed to calculate gestational age and evaluate fetal growth curves (Hadlock & Intergrowth-21st) on a device-local level. We are committed to maintaining your privacy and ensuring that no medical records, patient data, or personal details are collected, stored, or transmitted.

1. No Data Collection (Personal & Medical)

The application does not collect, record, process, store, or transmit any type of personal patient data or health-related information. All inputs (such as Last Menstrual Period dates, Ultrasound dates, visit dates, and gestational ages or fetal weight measurements) are held layout-only in the temporary working memory (RAM) of your device and are immediately cleared when the application is closed or reset.

Specifically, we do NOT collect, store, or share:

  • Patient names or clinical identifiers
  • Clinical dates (LMP, Ultrasound Dates, Visit Dates)
  • Fetal growth inputs and results (Gestational Age, Estimated Fetal Weight, Percentiles)
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or network details
  • Location data or GPS coordinates

2. No User Accounts

The application does not support or maintain any user accounts or user registration. No login credentials, profile setups, or backend connections are used or required to access any functionality of the application.

3. Data Security and Privacy

Because the application operates fully offline and does not contain network sharing logic, there is no threat of medical data breach, transmission interception, or unauthorized server access. You retain 100% control over the inputs. Medical practitioners can confidently use the calculator knowing that no patient Health Information (PHI) is ever exposed online.

4. Third-Party Services

This application does not use, embed, or integrate with any third-party SDKs or cloud services, including:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics networks (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • Cloud databases or remote backup servers
  • Third-party crash reporting tools that collect identifiers

5. Device Permissions

The application does not request access to hardware sensors, camera, microphone, local storage, contacts, or location services. It does not require any permission inputs to function.

6. Children's Privacy

We comply with COPPA, GDPR, and other international children's privacy regulations. As the app does not collect any data, there is no risk of capturing clinical or personal information from child patients or children under 13.

7. Open-Source Notice

This application's source code is part of the "One Week One App" project block publicly maintained under the developer’s repository. The guarantees described here apply strictly to the official version distributed on the app store by Dr. Sapto Labs.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect, store, or process any of your personal data on any server, there is no database record for us to access, rectify, delete, or export. Your data remains fully under your physical control.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect development practices. Any updates will be pushed directly to this web location and will be accessible via our App Store and Google Play Console store listings.
