Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the “dr. Sapto Anthro” application handles data. Our application is a professional anthropometric analysis and toddler growth tracking tool. It is built as an offline-first storage and analysis utility where all calculations, database records, and speech features are executed entirely on your device. We are committed to maintaining your privacy and ensuring that no child's patient information, photos, or geographical tags are ever collected, stored, or transmitted to any external servers.

1. Offline-First Processing and Data Safety

The application does not collect, record, store, or transmit any type of personal patient data. All patient growth history records, calculations (Z-scores and percentiles), and metadata are processed and saved solely in your device's secure local SQLite database. Your data remains entirely on your device, and we do not have access to any records you create.

Specifically, we do NOT collect or upload:

  • Child clinical data (names, birth dates, growth measurements, Z-scores, status classifications)
  • Parents information (mother's name, father's name, addresses)
  • Photos or local profile pictures
  • Coordinates/GPS locations tagged in the app
  • Voice transcriptions from the assistant

2. Device Permissions and Usage

Camera & Gallery: Used exclusively to allow you to capture or choose a profile photo for the child's offline growth record. These files are saved locally and never sent over any network connection.

Location Services (GPS): Used exclusively to allow clinical practitioners to tag the local home coordinates of patient visits for geographical statistics. All coordinates are stored locally in the database and never uploaded.

Microphone (Voice Assistant): Used to enable hands-free voice input of measurements (e.g., age, weight, height). The voice transcription is handled entirely through the local system API and is not recorded or shared.

3. Data Security

Because the application is fully offline and does not transmit data to external servers, the risk of data interception or server-side breach is eliminated. Your patients' records remain entirely on your device and are subject to your device's built-in security features. As a clinical practitioner, the privacy of patient records is secured by your device's physical security and screen lock.

4. Third-Party Services

This application does not use or integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • Cloud storage or backup solutions
  • Crash reporting systems that capture user identifiers

5. Children's Privacy

We comply with COPPA and other international child privacy regulations. Since no data is collected, there is no risk of children's clinical growth information being captured, tracked, or stored by us or any third parties.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no data to access, modify, delete, or export from our end. You maintain full clinical control over your patient profiles and are free to export records locally to Excel files for your own records or administrative needs.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in development practices. Updates will be posted in this document and accessible through the app store listing.
